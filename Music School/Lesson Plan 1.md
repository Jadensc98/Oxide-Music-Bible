### Workflow behind producing
- Ideation: Coming up with ideas, writing chords and melodies, designing sounds
	  These are the beginning stages of writing music. The moments you are messing around with sounds and melodies are some of the most important.

- Production: Writing beats and rhythms, layering instruments and sounds, creating the vibe of the track.
	  This is where the actual "producing" begins. Once you have an idea from the ideation stage, it's time to bring your ideas to life through the selection of sounds.
	  
- Arrangement: Structuring the song in time, Creating intro, verse, chorus, etc., building tension and excitement
	  Once you have the sounds you need, you now have to arrange them in the structure of a song. 
	  
- Mixing: Blending all sounds smoothly, Applying effects and fixing problems, making everything sound clear.
